# Who Drafdeck serves

These are working hypotheses for teams of two to five students. Five interviews have not taken place.

| Person | Three jobs | Three frustrations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team lead | Identify the binding rules and submission deadline. Divide deliverables among available teammates. Check evidence and own the final submission. | Conflicting dates across a poster and rulebook. Re-entering the same information in different screens. Discovering missing evidence on submission night. |
| Team member | Find today's assignment. Ask for help in context. Hand over finished work with evidence. | Reading a whole group chat to find one decision. Losing edits on unreliable mobile data. Seeing estimates and AI advice without knowing their source. |

## Proposed cuts

Keep five primary destinations. Put files and source references in Rules, planning and task execution in Work, members and conversations in Team, and delivery evidence in Submit. Offer usage through the header and show recent team activity inline. Retire the standalone Learning, Portfolio export, History, Notifications and AI Credits surfaces only following replacement verification as approved by the owner. Existing project records, account access, attachments and private conversations must survive the transition. Keep the advanced tools reachable while their replacement is incomplete.

## Interview guide

Recruit two leads and three members, including someone who works mainly on a phone and someone who reads a regional-language rulebook. Give each ten minutes. Ask them to find the submission time and cite the page, identify their next task, then locate a decision and attach delivery evidence. Record time, wrong turns, missing information and their own words with permission. Reconsider each cut against observed failures.
